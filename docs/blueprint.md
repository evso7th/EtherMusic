# **App Name**: EtherMusic

## Core Features:

- Melody Theremin: Melody Theremin: A virtual theremin interface to generate melodies by adjusting pitch and volume via mouse or touch.
- Bass Theremin: Bass Theremin: A second virtual theremin dedicated to creating basslines, with controls optimized for lower frequencies.
- Beat-Box: Beat-Box: A simple drum machine with pre-loaded drum patterns, featuring controls for tempo and volume. Includes common beat patterns and individual drum/cymbal sounds.
- Playback Controls: Playback Controls: Standard playback controls (play, pause, stop, record) for the theremin and beat-box.
- Sound Synthesis: Sound Synthesis: Implementation of the theremin sounds, synthesizing audio waveforms based on user input.
- Audio Mixing: Audio Mixing: Mixing of the sounds generated by both Theremins with the drum beats from Beat-Box

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep electric purple (#8A2BE2), reminiscent of the vastness of space where ethereal theremin sounds might resonate.
- Background color: Near-black, very desaturated purple (#1A051F) to enhance the vibrancy of other elements and reduce eye strain during prolonged use.
- Accent color: A cool, slightly desaturated cyan (#00B7EB) to draw attention to interactive elements and highlight specific audio parameters.
- Font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for both headlines and user interface, with its techy feel reinforcing the theremin app's computerized design.
- Abstract, glowing icons that symbolize sound waves, frequencies, and musical controls. Use the accent color to make them stand out.
- Theremin controls placed at the top of the interface, while the beat-box sits at the bottom. Mixing tools will be available from a sidebar.
- Subtle animations, like a pulse, on audio waveforms to visualize the sound being created.
